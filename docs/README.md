## The One-Paragraph Pitch

ANRIX is a multi-sided marketplace + professional network + project-management toolkit for the entire Indian construction value chain. Where Urban Company stops at home services, MagicBricks at listings, IndiaMART at materials, Naukri at jobs, and LinkedIn at professional identity — ANRIX unifies all five into a single role-aware super-app, with AI matching and GPS discovery as the connective tissue, and a trust/escrow layer that makes high-value construction transactions safe.

## Competitive Frame

| Competitor | What they own | What ANRIX absorbs |
|------------|---------------|--------------------|
| Urban Company | On-demand vetted services | Worker hiring marketplace + availability + attendance |
| MagicBricks / 99acres | Property listings | Buy/sell property module |
| IndiaMART | B2B materials | Material marketplace + equipment rental |
| Naukri | Job board | Construction jobs marketplace + bidding |
| LinkedIn | Professional identity & network | Verified pro profiles, portfolios, reviews, network |

**The wedge:** none of them serve the *daily-wage worker ↔ contractor ↔ builder* relationship with hiring, attendance, and payments in one loop. That's the under-served core ANRIX starts from.
